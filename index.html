How it works

Four simple steps from your first message to a well-run event.

  1. Tell us about your eventSend the date, venue, type of event and the size of team you need.
  2. Get your quoteWe confirm the package and price, adjusted to your budget.
  3. Brief and planYour Event Team Leader meets you to agree on guest flow and special guests.
  4. We run the dayOur uniformed ushers guide guests so you can enjoy your event.

Why Wazing

Too many events are poorly organised, or ushered by people with no training. Wazing is a team of young, disciplined professionals who show up on time, dress the part and treat every guest with respect.

The name comes from a popular sheng word for friends, because we want every client to feel looked after by friends who take the work seriously.

Our vision: to become one of the most trusted youth-driven enterprises in Kenya.

Our code of conduct

Every member agrees to:

  • Arrive on time for briefings and events
  • Wear the official Wazing attire and keep a neat appearance
  • Stay sober and professional on duty
  • Follow the Event Team Leader and Operations Manager
  • Keep client information confidential
  • Protect the Wazing name and identity

Questions people ask

Can you work at a small event?

Yes. We serve all events and gatherings, small to big. Use the hourly rate for short events (minimum 2 hours).

Are the prices fixed?

No. The prices shown are a guide and can be adjusted to suit your budget and request.

Do you only do weddings, funerals, corporate events and birthdays?

Those are our main events, but we work at any occasion and follow the directions of the event owner.

Do you offer errand running?

It is coming soon, mainly staffed by university students. Tell us if you are interested and we will share rates when it launches.

Where do you work?

We are flexible and we offer our services to you wherever you are.
